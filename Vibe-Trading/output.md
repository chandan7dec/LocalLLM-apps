**Stock Report: Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)**

**Overview**

Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) is a technology giant that has been a leader in the industry for decades. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, MSFT has grown to become one of the largest and most profitable companies in the world. The company's diverse portfolio includes operating systems (Windows), productivity software (Office), and cloud computing services (Azure).
